Source: Byteex – Standard Development Test, desktop 1:1166, mobile 1:1633.

## Observed layout
- Desktop: approximately 1440px-wide long landing page; mobile approximately 428px.
- Slim cream announcement, black BYTEEX wordmark. Desktop logo left; mobile centered.
- Hero: two columns; navy heading and three small icon benefits on left; three portrait photos with cream backdrop on right. CTA and overlapping white review card below.
- Mobile hero: heading, photo collage, benefits, full-width CTA, review.
- Cream fading press strip: five gray publication logos; mobile carousel.
- Benefits: heading and four icon/text rows on left, portrait gallery on right. Mobile: heading, gallery, benefits, CTA.
- Pale gray story block: offset three-photo composition and text. Mobile: heading, composition, text.
- Three process cards: save, ship, enjoy. Mobile horizontal carousel. Navy CTA with gold review stars.
- Reviews: heading, introduction, two rows of customer photos; three review cards desktop, swipeable cards mobile.
- FAQ: accordion left, staggered photos right; mobile hides photos and adds CTA.
- Pale gray impact strip: three columns desktop; mobile displays first two stacked.
- Final CTA: heading, intro, three-photo collage, button and review stars, cream gradient at bottom.
- No price selector or checkout is visible in these supplied landing-page frames. CTAs link to the requested product listing; no invented checkout is added.

Initial sizes/colors are estimates from the rendered frames, not claimed exact Figma values. Figma photos are downloaded from observed signed asset URLs; no stock substitutes are used. Font match and vector icons need final confirmation against authenticated Figma inspection.
